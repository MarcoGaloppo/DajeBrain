\section{Problem 1 GED}

To approach the problem of being a snitch with respect to $100$ green eyed dragons I think it is better to reduce the scale first. 

What if there was a single (possibly at this point insane) dragon and I told them \lq\lq Hey, at least one of you has green eyes\rq\rq. They would be confused (since it is only one). And would also now that they have for sure green eyes. And off to being a sparrow they go (whilst being pissy at me). 

What if there are only two dragons now? I still snitch and say the same. Both dragons know that the other has green eyes. Now. Dragon Peppe knows that dragon Francesca has green eyes, and that dragon Francesca sees his eyes color. Both are logical. Logically, if dragon Francesca did not see dragon Peppe as green-eyed, she would know that she had green eyes and would need to go and transform. And the other way around for dragon Peppe with respect to dragon Francesca. They do not talk about eye color of course. But as midnight strikes and neither fulfills their sacred oath...SHOCK! They now both know that they must have green eyes. And thus, the next day they both transform at midnight. Sad ending unlocked. 

Now, what happens when three dragons are there. Franesca, Peppe and Andrea. Well. From Peppe knows that Francesca and Andrea are gree-eyed. He also knows that Francesca also knows that Andrea is green-eyed as they both see him. And he knows that Andrea also sees Francesca as green-eyed. They are now all told that "at least one is green eyed". They do not talk about eye color at all. So no one mentions anything more.
